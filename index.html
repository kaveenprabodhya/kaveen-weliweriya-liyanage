01 / ABOUT

About me

I am an emerging AI researcher with an MSc in Applied Artificial Intelligence (Distinction) from the University of Warwick and a First Class BSc (Hons) in Computing from Coventry University.

My research interests lie at the intersection of affective computing, user modelling and human-centred generative AI, exploring how intelligent systems can account for human emotions, behaviour and individual differences to support more personalised experiences.

Research experience

MSC DISSERTATION · WARWICK · 2026

Affective Sonic Agents for Emotion Alignment

Developed a multi-agent framework to investigate whether generated sonic logos align with intended emotional targets and how synthetic audience ratings vary across personality profiles. The dissertation pipeline combines valence–arousal modelling, independent emotion estimators and 32 OCEAN-based audience personas.

Source code

Explore 96 audio stimuli across 16 brand briefs and 48 repeated generation runs in the dissertation’s public listening gallery.

BSC DISSERTATION · 2024

Emotionally Resonant Branding

Developed an AI-driven prototype combining facial-emotion recognition, emotion–colour mapping and generative imagery for music branding. Conducted a quantitative study with 191 music listeners, musicians and producers to investigate acceptance, usefulness, trust and ethical concerns.
