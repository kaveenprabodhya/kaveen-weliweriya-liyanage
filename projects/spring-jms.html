How it is structured

What this demonstrates

  • Two messaging styles: ordinary queued delivery and request-reply are both implemented, making the communication semantics visible.
  • Typed message conversion: payload classes are serialised through a configured Jackson converter rather than hand-parsing strings in every listener.
  • Spring-managed scheduling and listening: producers and consumers are wired through framework lifecycle features instead of bespoke threads.
  • Self-contained runtime: an embedded Artemis broker makes the messaging flow reproducible without a separately installed broker.
